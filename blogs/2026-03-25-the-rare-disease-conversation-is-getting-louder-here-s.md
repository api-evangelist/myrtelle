Driven by a surge in novel scientific advancements, rare diseases have moved to the center of the national conversation. Researchers, policymakers, biotech leaders, and patient advocates are aligned on one uncomfortable truth: the system designed to get treatments to patients isn’t moving fast enough — and for families living with conditions like Canavan disease, that urgency is not theoretical.
