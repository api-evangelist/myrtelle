Every year, on the last day of February, the world joins hands for Rare Disease Day. For most, it is a day of awareness; for the Canavan disease community, it is a day of profound significance. Rare diseases, by definition, affect fewer than 1 in 2,000 people, but collectively, over 300 million people worldwide are fighting these “silent” battles.
