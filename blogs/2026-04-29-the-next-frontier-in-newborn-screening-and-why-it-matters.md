In gene therapy, timing isn’t a preference, it’s the treatment. The earlier a child can be diagnosed with Canavan disease, and a potential treatment initiated, the more of their future we can protect. Therefore, to maximize efficacy, the treatment window begins at birth to minimize neuronal loss to the disease.
